Television Energy Consumption

Modern televisions vary widely in power consumption based on screen dimensions, display technologies (e.g., LED, OLED, QLED), and onboard brightness controls.

This section explores television energy efficiency ratings and provides comparative data to guide consumer purchasing decisions.

Television Efficiency Guidelines

When selecting a television, consider the following energy efficiency factors:

  • Screen Size: Power draw scales rapidly as screen area increases.
  • Star Rating: Higher star ratings signify more energy-efficient backlighting and processing components.
  • Standby Power: Modern smart TVs draw continuous power when waiting for network signals or remote wake commands.
