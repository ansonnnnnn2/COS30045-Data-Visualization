Understanding Appliance Energy Consumption

Welcome to the Appliance Energy Consumption portal. This website provides data-driven insights into everyday domestic electrical appliances, helping consumers make smarter, more sustainable choices for their homes.

Explore our analysis on home appliances to learn how star ratings, device sizing, and operational efficiency impact your annual household energy costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

The Energy Rating Label displays a star rating along with estimated annual kilowatt-hour (kWh) usage, allowing consumers to compare running costs before making a purchase.

Appliance size, screen brightness, display backlighting technologies, and continuous standby power draw are the primary contributors to annual electricity consumption.

Electricity tariffs vary by region and consumption tier. For standard residential estimates, rates typically fall between 25 to 55 sen per kWh.
